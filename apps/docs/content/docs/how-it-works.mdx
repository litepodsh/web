title: How It Works
description: Understand how Litepod simplifies deployments on your own server.
---

Litepod sits on your server and gives you a focused control plane for deploying applications and databases without building a platform from scratch. Caddy handles reverse proxying and can obtain SSL certificates from Let's Encrypt for your domains.

## The deployment flow

1. **Connect your server.** Use infrastructure that you rent and control.
2. **Choose a service.** Select the application or database you want to run.
3. **Provide configuration.** Add the settings the service needs to start correctly.
4. **Deploy.** Start the deployment through the Litepod interface.
5. **Route traffic securely.** Caddy acts as the reverse proxy and can obtain SSL certificates through Let's Encrypt.
6. **Manage from one place.** Keep deployment work visible and repeatable.

## What stays in your hands

Litepod simplifies the deployment workflow. You still control the server, network, data, access, and operational policies around your services.

That balance gives you a lightweight experience without giving up ownership of your infrastructure.
